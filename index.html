01; The problem

You're getting stronger. It isn't showing up.

You lift three times a week and the numbers go up. Then round three arrives, your legs are gone, your rear hand is slow, and the shot that was supposed to land doesn't.

The lifting isn't wrong. It's just built for someone whose sport is lifting.

Force you can't express fast is decoration. Rotation you can't produce under fatigue doesn't exist in a fight. And a programme that ignores the six hours a week you already spend on the mats is a programme competing with your own training for the same recovery.
